## 1. Overview & Policy

In compliance with the Data Governance & Audit Directive:
1. **Real Data Priority:** All predictive performance and benchmark evaluations must use verified, legally acquired external datasets with documented provenance and licenses.
2. **Synthetic Data Quarantine:** Synthetic datasets are maintained strictly as unit test fixtures, schema validation examples, or integration stubs. They are explicitly labelled and separated at the filesystem, manifest, and documentation levels.
3. **Download Budget Enforcement:** Total download budget capped at 10 GiB; no single dataset exceeds 10 GB.

## 4. Privacy & Personal Data Assessment

- **No Personally Identifiable Information (PII):** Neither the EMSCAD dataset, MMLU dataset, nor ICAI regulatory corpus contains live personal resumes, private contact details, or candidate IDs.
- **Fair Dealing & Compliance:** All statutory texts are public Indian legislative gazettes. EMSCAD is CC0-1.0 public domain. MMLU is MIT licensed.
