## 4. Policy on External Stock Imagery & Logos

1. **No Fake User Avatars:** Profile avatars are either initialized with the authenticated user's genuine initials or real uploaded images. Stock model photos are strictly prohibited.
2. **No Fake Employer Logos:** Employer listings show verified text typography or official registered firm insignia where authorized. Unrelated stock imagery is never substituted for corporate identities.
3. **Zero Hallucinated Customer Testimonials:** Testimonial carousels with fake personas, invented names, or stock headshots are strictly banned across both public and authenticated views.
